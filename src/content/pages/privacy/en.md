h1: What this site keeps
lede: This is a personal site. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking scripts. Here is everything it keeps or sees, and where.
updated: "Last updated 8 November 2026"
sections:
  - - In your browser
    - "The site remembers a few choices on your device: the skin you picked and the skins you found (fx-skin, fx-skins), the CV language (fx-cv), whether you found the secret page (fx-secret), and the site language in a cookie named lang. Only the language cookie is sent back to the site, so it can answer in your language. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes all of it."
  - - Games and lab experiments
    - "Games and experiments run in your browser. An experiment can save its runs on your device, in this site’s browser storage, so you can compare them later. They stay there until you delete them or clear the site’s data. Nothing is uploaded."
  - - Hosting and logs
    - "The site runs on Cloudflare. To deliver a page, it receives your IP address and your browser’s request, like any web server. Cloudflare keeps request logs for this site (the address without its query string, the time, the result and technical details of the request) for a few days at most, and I use them only to find errors. Cloudflare’s privacy policy describes how it handles this data."
  - - No analytics
    - "No analytics, advertising or social media scripts are loaded, and no profile of you is built. Project data from GitHub is fetched when the site is built, not from your browser."
  - - Signing in to separate sites
    - "Some separate sites under oztozlu.dev are open only to people I invite. They use Cloudflare Access: you sign in with a one-time code sent to your email address, or with Google. With Google, your name, email address and profile picture are shared with Cloudflare Access; only the email address is used, to check it against that site’s list. After you sign in, Cloudflare sets a cookie on that site (CF_Authorization) and keeps a record of the sign-in. Sites protected by a password keep the key you entered only in that tab until you lock the site or close the tab."
  - - Email
    - "If you write to izzet@oztozlu.dev, I receive your message and address and use them only to reply. You can ask me to delete them."
  - - Questions
    - "Ask anything about this page at izzet@oztozlu.dev."
